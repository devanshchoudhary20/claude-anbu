# 🥋 DOJO — Senior Frontend Interview Syllabus

Target: 3 to 5 years experience, senior loops at FAANG, AI startups, India product companies (Flipkart, Atlassian, Razorpay, Uber, Swiggy, Cred). Budget: 14 hours a week, open-ended. Twelve modules, about twelve weeks, then rolling mocks.

## Weekly rhythm (14 hours)

| Slot | Hours | What |
|---|---|---|
| Concept module | 6 | The week's module: probe, build, review. Two 90-minute sessions plus one 3-hour weekend block. |
| Machine coding | 4 | Two timed 60-minute problems from module 04's pool, each followed by a 30-minute review. Starts week 2 and never stops. |
| DSA light | 1.5 | One problem from module 12, 45 minutes, plus review. |
| Reviews | 1.5 | Spaced retrieval of every `learned` item that is due. Daily, short. |
| Behavioral | 1 | One STAR story drafted or rehearsed from module 11. Starts week 3. |

## Modules

| # | Slug | Weeks | Panel it serves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | js-internals | 1-2 | every loop; the phone screen |
| 02 | browser | 3 | FAANG quiz round, India fundamentals round |
| 03 | react-internals | 4 | every React loop |
| 04 | machine-coding | 2 onward, interleaved | India product companies, startup pair rounds |
| 05 | system-design | 5-6 | FAANG senior gate, Atlassian, Razorpay |
| 06 | performance | 7 | FAANG, e-commerce (Flipkart, Swiggy) |
| 07 | typescript | 8 (half) | startups, Atlassian |
| 08 | architecture | 8 (half) - 9 | senior and lead signals everywhere |
| 09 | accessibility | 9, interleaved into 04 | FAANG UI rounds decide modal and dropdown here |
| 10 | ai-frontend | 10 | AI startups |
| 11 | behavioral | 3 onward, interleaved | senior loops fail here more than on code |
| 12 | dsa-light | 2 onward, interleaved | FAANG screens, India round 1 |

Weeks 11-12: rolling mocks across all rounds, weakest first.

## Pass bar language

Every module file ends with a pass bar: a list of items the ledger tracks. An item is `learned` when the build met the bar and the user answered the follow-ups without hints. A module is complete when every item is `learned` and has one review scored 4 or above.

## SM-2-lite scheduling

Score each retrieval 0 to 5. 5: instant, complete, with the edge case. 4: correct, one nudge. 3: correct, slow or missing the edge case. 2 and below: miss.

| Score | Next interval |
|---|---|
| 5 | previous interval x 2.5 (1d, 3d, 7d, 18d, 45d) |
| 4 | previous interval x 2 |
| 3 | repeat the previous interval |
| 0-2 | 1 day, and after three misses the item returns to the build queue |

## Session shape

Probe, then build, then review, then schedule. Ninety minutes. The sensei asks first, explains last, and never writes the solution.
